# TExporT for Android — Privacy Policy

*Effective 2026-10-11 · American Milestone Inc*

TExporT for Android does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- **What it receives:** only text you choose to send to it. That means text you select and mark with "Mark with TExporT", or text you share to it. It also records which app the text came from (for example "Claude"), so your snippets are grouped by app.
- **Where it goes:** into the app's private storage on your phone. The app requests **no permissions**, including no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- **Exports** (Markdown, `.texport.json`) are created only when you tap Share or Save, and go only where you send or save them.
- **Deleting your data:** delete collections or markers in the app, or uninstall it.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no ads.**
